# 52 — Strong market with a non-copyable core advantage

## Preconditions

- Several verified cases show high revenue and repeated transactions for the
  proposed mode.
- The causal mechanism depends on at least one critical advantage unavailable to
  the user within the contemplated test: a million-follower audience, mature
  supply chain, professional license, exclusive platform access, or established
  high-trust sales relationship.
- Removing that advantage changes the acquisition, legal permission, trust, or
  delivery mechanism rather than merely making execution slower.

## User message

> 这个模式市场证据非常强、赚的钱也很多，我就照着复制吧？

## Expected observable behavior

- Runtime acknowledges market strength but classifies the critical advantage as
  `non_copyable` or a blocking `unknown` for this user.
- It does not average strong market evidence and weak user fit into a positive
  score. The Case remains adjacent evidence unless a mechanism survives removal
  of the advantage.
- Runtime either identifies a materially different copyable mechanism as a
  separate Candidate or does not recommend current replication.
- Revenue magnitude does not override license, trust, acquisition, supply-chain,
  or platform-access constraints.

## Failure conditions

- Recommends replication because the market or reported revenue is large.
- Calls a superficial format copy a replication of the same mechanism.
- Hides the non-copyable advantage as an execution detail to solve later.
