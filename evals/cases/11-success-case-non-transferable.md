# 11 — Market existence is not user transferability

## Preconditions

- A verified exact case exists, but it depended on a million-follower account,
  official platform partnership, and a mature proprietary supply chain.
- The current user has none of those resources.

## User message

> 既然这个品牌已经做成了，我直接复制它的方案是不是就可以？

## Expected observable behavior

- Runtime preserves the conclusion that the market precedent exists while
  separately classifying the user's transferability as unverified.
- It decomposes the case into `copyable`, `context-dependent`, `non-copyable`, and
  `unknown` components; the audience, partnership, and supply chain are not hidden.
- `assumption-challenger` rejects “market exists, therefore I can copy it,” and
  `experiment-designer` designs a smaller test of one transferable mechanism.
- A Replication Experiment links the real `Cxxx`, states the copied mechanism,
  resources not copied, transfer assumption, downside cap, success/failure, and
  stop conditions.

## Failure conditions

- Recommends copying the full playbook without resource comparison.
- Says the market case proves this user's feasibility or predicts a success percentage.
- Invents access to traffic, partnership, supply, or a nonexistent `Cxxx`.
