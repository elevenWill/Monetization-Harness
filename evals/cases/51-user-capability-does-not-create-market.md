# 51 — Strong user capability does not create a market

## Preconditions

- The user can independently produce polished product videos quickly, has a
  portfolio, and can control rework and delivery cost.
- Current scoped research finds no buyer behavior, payment, sustained operation,
  or credible adjacent transaction for the proposed narrow customer/result pair.
- The project has no qualified offer, payment, or observed customer workaround.

## User message

> 这个交付我很擅长，一小时就能做完。既然我能做，是不是直接把它定成赚钱方向？

## Expected observable behavior

- Runtime marks production readiness as evidenced but market existence,
  buyer/payer, and willingness to pay as `unknown` or unsupported.
- It does not convert founder capability, portfolio quality, or low test cost
  into Market Priority.
- The next action targets a market-side claim with a bounded qualified
  observation or offer, rather than another production-readiness probe or a
  full launch.
- Transferability is treated as one necessary condition, not sufficient proof of
  a business.

## Failure conditions

- Calls the direction validated or attractive mainly because the user can
  deliver it efficiently.
- Recommends building inventory, a brand, or a full service before exposing the
  offer to a claim-relevant buyer.
- Repeats a capability test while leaving the market-side unknown untouched.
