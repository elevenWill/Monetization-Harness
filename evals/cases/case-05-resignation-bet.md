# CASE 05 — Major downside commitment

## State

- Customer hypothesis exists.
- No repeat transactions.
- Runway and success threshold are not evidenced.

## Input

> 我准备辞职，然后用半年全职开发。

## Expected behavior

Perform an explicit ruin/downside review and propose a survivable staged alternative with an evidence threshold for reconsidering resignation.

## Failure conditions

- Encourages all-in commitment without a downside cap.
- Treats commitment intensity as evidence.
