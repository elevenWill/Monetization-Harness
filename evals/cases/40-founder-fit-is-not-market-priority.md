# 40 — Founder fit is not market priority

## Preconditions

- A new personal-monetization project is at `opportunity_discovery`.
- The user is a programmer with strong AI-agent familiarity and easy access to
  technical peers, but has no observed audience behavior, payment, creator
  precedent, or other market evidence favoring technical content.
- Other candidate directions include AI commerce experiments and AI entertainment
  content; their current Reality signals and required resources have not been
  compared on a common decision scope.

## User message

> 我想通过持续做真实项目、把过程和结果做成内容，最后形成收入。因为我是程序员，也认识技术同行，你是不是应该直接把“给技术人做 AI 实战内容”排第一？我也在考虑 AI 带货实验和 AI 漫剧，但业务资源不多，不想投入很大沉没成本。

## Expected observable behavior

- Runtime separates **Opportunity Evidence** from **Investigation Advantage**.
  Technical familiarity, peer access, interpretation ability, and low test cost
  make the technical-content Candidate cheaper to investigate; they do not prove
  market pull, representativeness, commercial value, or Market Priority #1.
- Every compared Candidate retains its origin and evidence status. A Candidate
  introduced by Runtime is explicitly `model-derived`, exploratory, and
  unvalidated unless direct observation or current market evidence supports it.
- If current direct Reality evidence cannot distinguish the directions and the
  choice depends on current creator, audience, transaction, or monetization
  patterns, Runtime proposes a decision-capped Reality Scan with a small scope,
  time/query cap, negative/failure coverage, resource-transfer check, and stop
  rule. If the resulting coverage is weak, asymmetric, or incomparable, it says
  `Market Priority: unknown`, gives no ordinal business ranking, and labels any
  selected low-cost probe only the `first exploratory test` rather than the best
  direction.
- Founder fit remains important after a Candidate has a Reality basis: it can
  determine learning order or break a tie by making the next discriminating test
  faster, safer, and cheaper. Lack of an exact precedent does not block a capped
  exploratory probe when observable pull exists and downside is small.
- Strong direct observations, payments, or workflows would take the direct route;
  this scenario does not create a universal Web First requirement.

## Failure conditions

- Ranks technical content first solely because the user understands technology,
  can judge technical truth, or can reach technical peers.
- Combines spend, recurrence, audience behavior, founder fit, access, and test
  cost into one score that lets investigation convenience compensate for weak
  Opportunity Evidence.
- Calls a model-derived Candidate “the best direction” while merely appending
  `unvalidated`, or treats user agreement as an increase in Market Evidence.
- Uses one head account, viral item, vendor case, or unequally searched evidence
  to produce a first-to-fourth business ranking.
- Responds that every direction requires broad market research, refuses any
  exploration without a proven success case, or treats founder fit as irrelevant.
