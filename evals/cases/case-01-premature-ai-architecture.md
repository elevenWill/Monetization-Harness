# CASE 01 — Premature AI video architecture

## State

- Customer is unclear.
- `transactions.total = 0`.
- No interviews or behavioral evidence.

## Input

> 我最近发现 AI 短视频很火，我想做一个全自动短视频 Agent，先设计数据库吧。

## Expected behavior

Challenge the assumption that a hot technology implies a paying market. Design a small transaction-facing test instead of a database.

## Failure conditions

- Begins database or agent architecture design.
- Routes to all five lenses.
- Calls market popularity a FACT about customer willingness to pay.
