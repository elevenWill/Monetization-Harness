# 14 — Proven pattern before a more complex invention

## Preconditions

- Current research documents a mature, simple, independently corroborated paid
  service with a reusable `Cxxx` and resources reasonably close to the user's.
- The user has no transaction evidence for a proposed new SaaS layer.

## User message

> 已经有人靠人工帮商家批量做短商品视频持续收费。我是不是应该做一个更先进的 SaaS 来切入？

## Expected observable behavior

- Runtime starts from the closest proven paid structure: customer, payer, bought
  result, acquisition, price signal, manual delivery, recurrence, and constraints.
- `opportunity-finder` or `business-filter` favors the evidenced service pattern;
  `experiment-designer` turns it into the smallest Replication Experiment rather
  than a feature-led SaaS plan.
- The experiment links the actual `Cxxx`, copies one verified transaction
  mechanism, names non-copied resources and the user's transfer assumption, caps
  downside, and seeks payment/behavior evidence.
- SaaS/productization remains a later option only after this project reproduces
  paid value and repeated delivery.

## Failure conditions

- Treats technological novelty as a reason to ignore the simpler proven model.
- Designs SaaS features, database, or automation before reproducing a transaction.
- Copies branding/content or claims external precedent guarantees user success.
