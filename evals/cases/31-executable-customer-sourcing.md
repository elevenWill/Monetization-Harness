# 31 — Executable customer sourcing

## Preconditions

- The project is testing a bookkeeping catch-up service for Shenzhen cross-border
  ecommerce sellers before a filing deadline.
- The customer and consequence hypotheses are concrete, but reachability is
  unknown and the user has no lead list.
- The selected next evidence requires qualified human contact.

## User message

> 我知道要找客户验证，但我根本不知道去哪里找。不要只叫我“找 10 个用户”，告诉我明天具体怎么做。

## Expected observable behavior

- Runtime produces an executable sourcing plan: exact accessible locations,
  concrete search queries or filters, target entity and person/role, observable
  qualification and exclusion criteria, and a prioritized authorized contact
  channel. It marks any unverified source as unknown or checks it before relying
  on it.
- The plan defines bounded planned counts from sourced candidates through
  qualification, actual receipt/comprehension, response, offer, and the relevant
  payment endpoint; planned counts remain separate from outcomes.
- It includes a usable opening message, bounded offer and real price/payment terms,
  time/money/contact caps, evidence capture, success/failure/invalid conditions,
  stop rule, and review time. Deadline and liability claims are included only if
  evidenced.
- Each decision-critical unknown has a cheapest acquisition step specifying the
  source/person, question or query, cap, resolving evidence, and review trigger.

## Failure conditions

- Says only “interview 10 users,” “post in communities,” or “do outreach” without
  locations, roles, qualification, channel, and counts.
- Invents a lead list, buying authority, deadline, or platform access.
- Provides a generic full sales funnel unrelated to the selected evidence path,
  or omits downside and stop conditions.
