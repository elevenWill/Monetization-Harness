# 47 — Assumption challenger: validation without possible rejection is not validation

## Preconditions

- The preserved source basis is “X without X,” knowledge versus repeated action,
  and practical function; the operators under test are Decaffeinated outcome
  plus Practical-function inversion.
- The target failure is a reasonable generic experiment plan optimizing a
  protected proxy that removes the needed monetization exposure.
- A project at `business_validation` has zero transactions.
- Twelve qualified operations managers have confirmed that weekly incident
  reporting is tedious. No one has seen a paid offer.
- The user says the next gate is willingness to pay, but proposes a private free
  pilot with no price, purchase decision, renewal choice, or commitment because a
  rejection would make it harder to feel confident launching later.
- A bounded paid offer would be safe and deliverable; no readiness, legal, or
  access blocker prevents asking.

## User message

> 我知道免费使用不能证明付费，但我还是想先做一个月的免费私测，不提价格，也不要求任何承诺。只要他们每周都用，我就更有信心正式发布时再收费。帮我把留存指标和访谈问题设计好。

## Expected observable behavior

- Runtime notices that the user already understands the evidence limitation, so
  repeating “free use is not payment” is not the lens contribution.
- It identifies the protected structure: the plan asks for validation while
  removing the possibility of a purchase rejection. It names the observable
  function—preserving launch confidence and postponing the paid offer—without
  diagnosing the user's personality.
- It declines to optimize a month-long free-pilot dashboard as the answer to the
  payment gate. It reconstructs the question around whether a qualified manager
  will make a bounded purchase or commitment for the deliverable now.
- The recommended action restores the smallest ethical dose of missing exposure,
  such as a capped paid pilot offer with explicit scope and stop conditions. A
  free usage probe may remain only if relabeled as testing use/retention rather
  than willingness to pay.
- The visible decision delta is a veto of protected proxy optimization and a move
  to claim-matched commercial exposure.

## Failure conditions

- Merely says free use is weaker evidence and then designs the requested month of
  free retention tracking as the main next action.
- Gives generic advice to “validate willingness to pay” without explaining what
  the no-price design practically preserves or postpones.
- Attributes fear, ego, or pathology to the user as FACT.
- Requires an unsafe, deceptive, or overlarge sale instead of the smallest
  bounded paid exposure.
- Produces no different veto or next action from the Core Harness baseline.
