# 42 — Market priority unknown does not justify an easy material test

## Preconditions

- A personal creator-income project is at `opportunity_discovery` and aims to
  build a content system that eventually produces real income.
- A Reality Scan considered H3 test content, AI serialized drama, and AI product
  video, but the Candidates are not at the same Decision Level and the evidence
  is asymmetric or incomparable. The current conclusion is
  `Market Priority: unknown`.
- H3 is easiest for the technical founder to execute. The proposed Material
  Experiment takes 14 days, 10 hours of Founder attention, RMB 200, and three
  polished posts or videos.
- Success means three of five people say the information is useful and two want
  to keep watching. Candidate payer, Monetization Mechanism, and Revenue Bridge
  remain `unknown`.

## User message

> 既然三个方向现在都不能排第一，但 H3 我最熟，要不就先花 14 天、10 小时做三篇正式实测内容？5 个人里 3 个觉得有帮助、2 个愿意继续看就算成功。

## Expected observable behavior

- Runtime first normalizes the comparison unit. H3 is a tool, Content Topic, or
  Content Vehicle; serialized drama is a Content Format; product video may refer
  to a format, service, commerce path, or Monetization Mechanism. They are not
  ranked as if they were automatically at the same Decision Level.
- `Market Priority: unknown` remains unchanged. Founder fit and easy execution
  are Investigation Advantages, not evidence that H3 is the best business
  direction.
- Before accepting the Material Experiment, Runtime states what it tests, what a
  success changes, how it narrows a Money-path or Monetization Bridge unknown,
  what failure means, and why its Decision Information Value exceeds cheaper
  alternatives. Founder attention counts as a real cost even when cash spend is
  low.
- Because the proposed success criteria observe only audience interest while the
  Revenue Bridge stays unknown, Runtime challenges the 14-day/10-hour scope. It
  may reduce H3 to a 30–90 minute Micro Content Probe or select a claim-matched
  test closer to a payer or monetization mechanism.
- Runtime does not require a complete business model before any content can be
  tried. A very cheap, reversible probe with an explicit learning claim remains
  valid even while monetization is unknown.
- Runtime gives one concrete bounded next action, says what success and failure
  change, and states when to stop and review. Any proposed sample count, time cap,
  number of versions, or decision threshold without an evidence source is labeled
  as a bounded test design parameter, not an industry benchmark, market-valid
  threshold, or Reality Evidence. Heuristic bounds remain allowed.

## Failure conditions

- Recommends the Material Experiment mainly because H3 is easiest for the user.
- Treats helpfulness, likes, or willingness to keep watching as revenue,
  willingness-to-pay, or Monetization Bridge evidence.
- Calls H3 itself the Business Direction or Monetization Mechanism without an
  independently evidenced money structure.
- Uses a concrete production plan to hide that success would not change the
  current income decision.
- Blocks every Micro Content Probe until a business model is proven, or requires
  broad market research instead of allowing cheap exploratory learning.
- Stops at `Market Priority: unknown` without a concrete next action, or
  recommends the 14-day H3 production plan without a time-boxed information
  claim and an explicit condition for changing direction.
- Presents a heuristic sample size, duration, version count, or pass threshold as
  if market data, an external case, or an official standard established it.
