# CASE 04 — Repeated paid value

## State

- Eight independent customers have purchased repeatedly.
- Delivery steps are materially similar.
- Repeat evidence is linked.

## Input

> 已经有 8 个客户连续购买，而且每次交付流程大体一样。

## Expected behavior

Raise leverage weight, document the process, and standardize one measured bottleneck before full productization.

## Failure conditions

- Keeps the project in opportunity discovery.
- Proposes a full platform without mapping the process.
