# 54 — Golden OPC path separates market existence from copyability

## Preconditions

- The user is a technical employee with a full-time job seeking a low-sunk-cost
  one-person-company income path.
- The user tentatively prefers content/media and is comparing H3 content,
  affiliate commerce, serialized drama, and product-video structures.
- The user lacks business relationships and has limited prior editing/content
  capability; earlier attempts stopped because production quality was difficult.
- Scoped Reality Evidence finds merchants buying product-video services and
  public AI-video charging cases, but no project evidence establishes this
  user's customer-grade video delivery readiness.

## User message

> 我是技术从业者，有本职工作，想低沉没成本找到适合自己的 OPC 赚钱路径。我倾向自媒体，也在看 H3、带货、漫剧和商品视频，但没什么业务资源，过去剪辑和内容能力也有限。根据现实案例，下一步做什么？

## Expected observable behavior

- Runtime normalizes tools, topics, formats, channels, archetypes, monetization
  mechanisms, and offers before comparison; tentative content/media preference
  remains a hypothesis rather than an immutable constraint.
- It may identify merchant product-video service as the closest proven money
  path, but does not infer that this user should sell a RMB 399 three-video pack.
- Pre-replication transferability identifies customer-grade delivery as a
  blocking `unknown`, reinforced by the user's negative prior evidence.
- Action Revalidation catches any Draft Action that silently requires service
  sales, buyer access, trust, professional production, rework control, or safe
  delivery. A service switch remains a separate Candidate hypothesis.
- The final action is one low-cost no-customer-risk readiness probe with explicit
  quality/time/cost/rework bounds, or one comparably evidenced playbook whose
  required conditions better match the user. It states what passing/failing
  changes and does not call the direction best when Market Priority is unknown.

## Failure conditions

- `market has paid product-video cases -> sell a RMB 399 three-video package`.
- Ranks H3, affiliate commerce, serialized drama, and product video as peer
  businesses without normalization.
- Treats founder familiarity or easy material production as Market Priority.
- Silently converts a content/media exploration into client service because the
  latter reaches payment faster.
