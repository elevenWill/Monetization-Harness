# 54 — Proven-path request must not default to cold discovery

## Preconditions

- The user is a technical employee with a full-time job seeking a low-sunk-cost
  one-person-company income path.
- The user tentatively prefers content/media and is comparing H3 content,
  affiliate commerce, serialized drama, and product-video structures.
- The user lacks business relationships and has limited prior editing/content
  capability; earlier attempts stopped because production quality was difficult.
- The user explicitly wants to copy a reality-validated income path rather than
  have an LLM invent a business model.
- Initial scoped Reality Evidence finds one current merchant Buyer Lead seeking
  AI product-video production, another long-term-cooperation claim, and many
  competing suppliers, but no credible payment/price, delivery, acceptance,
  acquisition, repeat, or economics evidence.

## User message

> 我是技术从业者，有本职工作，想低沉没成本找到适合自己的 OPC 赚钱路径。我倾向自媒体，也在看 H3、带货、漫剧和商品视频，但没什么业务资源，过去剪辑和内容能力也有限。我想先找现实已验证、可复制的钱路，不要让 LLM 发明商业模式。下一步做什么？

## Expected observable behavior

- Runtime normalizes tools, topics, formats, channels, archetypes, monetization
  mechanisms, and offers before comparison; tentative content/media preference
  remains a hypothesis rather than an immutable constraint.
- It selects the Transaction-led route: Buyer Transaction Surface -> seller/Case
  reconstruction -> Pattern -> Transferability -> Replication Probe. The initial
  post remains a Buyer Lead, not a transaction-bearing Case.
- Within a declared query/time/channel budget it searches continuing sellers and
  public offer, price/paid signal, acquisition, delivery, acceptance/rework,
  review, repeat/continuation, failure, and economics evidence.
- If the money skeleton still cannot be reconstructed, it states
  `playbook evidence blocked`, lists the exact missing fields, and selects the
  cheapest Agent-side public evidence source most likely to fill the decisive
  field. It does not tell the user to find or interview three merchants.
- Human Contact is allowed only after reasonable public routes are exhausted and
  asks one field-specific question. It does not restart broad customer discovery.
- If research instead finds a transaction-bearing Case, Runtime runs
  transferability. Customer-grade delivery remains a blocking `unknown`, so the
  next action is a no-customer-risk readiness probe or shadow delivery against a
  real public brief with quality/time/cost/rework bounds—not an RMB 399 live offer.
- It keeps `Market Priority: unknown` when the monetization structures remain
  incomparable and preserves Action Revalidation, Stage, evidence, and persistence
  boundaries.

## Failure conditions

- Stops after the Buyer Lead because it is enough for a first exploratory test.
- Tells the user to select three merchants and broadly ask about product, use
  case, current workflow, standards, and spend.
- Calls public playbook evidence blocked without naming missing fields or the
  cheapest field-matched source.
- `market has paid product-video cases -> sell a RMB 399 three-video package`.
- Ranks H3, affiliate commerce, serialized drama, and product video as peer
  businesses without normalization.
- Treats founder familiarity or easy material production as Market Priority.
- Silently converts a content/media exploration into client service because the
  latter reaches payment faster.
- Manufactures “AI product creative lab” or another unsupported business winner,
  then supplies production scaffolding such as five product facts, three scenes,
  three claim restrictions, pain/demo/objection variants, or a two-of-three pass
  rule without identifying those numbers as bounded test design parameters.
- Uses Investigation Advantage or easy reachability alone to allocate substantial
  founder attention to the weak transaction hypothesis.
