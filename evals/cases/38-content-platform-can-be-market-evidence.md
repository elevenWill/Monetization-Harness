# 38 — A content platform can also be a market-evidence source

## Preconditions

- A creator project is deciding which AI-content theme has observable audience
  pull and which creator transaction patterns already operate.
- The user can contact several technical friends, but that nearby sample is known
  to be narrow and low-paying.
- No fresh, scope-matched platform or creator-market research exists.

## User message

> 我现在是想判断什么 AI 内容有人持续看、哪些账号已经形成赞助或付费产品。是不是先别看小红书和 B站？它们只是以后发内容的渠道，我现在先问微信里的技术朋友就够了吧？

## Expected observable behavior

- Runtime rejects the permanent distribution-only framing. For a claim about
  content demand, audience pull, creator precedent, or monetization pattern, it
  recognizes that relevant content platforms can also be market observation
  environments containing behavioral, negative, competitive, and transaction
  clues.
- It chooses Reality Evidence by the current decision claim, cost, scope, and
  decision impact. A bounded platform study is appropriate here, while direct
  workflow interviews may still be better when the claim is only whether a
  technical problem occurs.
- Any platform evidence is calibrated: views, likes, followers, comments,
  sponsorship labels, vendor claims, and advertised products support only the
  claims their provenance allows. Runtime records inaccessible channels and
  proxy gaps and does not call platform activity proof of profit or transferability.
- The answer proposes a bounded evidence action rather than a compulsory broad
  web-first study, and may combine or sequence direct contact only when it answers
  a separate material claim.

## Failure conditions

- Defines Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, or other content platforms only as future
  distribution channels and excludes them from content-market evidence by rule.
- Uses nearby technical-friend interviews to answer audience-pull or creator-
  monetization claims without explaining the proxy gap.
- Turns the scenario into mandatory exhaustive platform research for every
  content project, or treats platform popularity metrics as validated demand,
  profit, payer identity, or this project's transferability.
- Runs research ceremonially without naming the decision claim it can change.
