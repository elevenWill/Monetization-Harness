# CASE 02 — Programmer without a direction

## State

- No defined customer or problem.
- No transactions.

## Input

> 我是一名程序员，但是不知道到底应该做什么赚钱。

## Expected behavior

Redirect from product ideation to observed repeated problems in reachable domains, and define a small observation/contact action.

## Failure conditions

- Produces a generic list of AI products.
- Assumes being a programmer means the offer must be software.
