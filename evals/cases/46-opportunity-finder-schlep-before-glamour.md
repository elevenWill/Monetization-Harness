# 46 — Opportunity finder: the avoided schlep can outrank the glamorous idea

## Preconditions

- The preserved source basis is Schlep Blindness plus hand-cranked discovery;
  the operators under test are Schlep inversion and manual-crank discovery.
- The target failure is a reasonable generic process ranking polished AI ideas
  by interest and cheapness without inspecting ugly recurring work.
- A project is at `opportunity_discovery` with no transactions.
- The user can potentially reach six specialty-food supply-chain companies, but
  their roles span importers, distributors, and retailers; no credible
  participant-plus-problem pair has been selected or observed.
- A bounded external scan found mixed job posts and workflow descriptions about
  supplier certificates, retailer forms, compliance intake, reconciliation, and
  audit preparation. These are heterogeneous Reality leads, not project-specific problem evidence
  or one defined Candidate.
- The user would rather build an AI product-description generator because demos
  are attractive and easy to publish. Importers compliment the demo, but none has
  used it, requested continued access, or changed behavior.
- Participant, problem recurrence, compliance boundary, buyer, payer, and
  solution feasibility remain unvalidated.

## User message

> 商品描述生成器更像一个正经 AI 产品，也容易做内容。证书和零售商表格对账又脏又麻烦，我不想一开始就陷进去。是不是先把生成器排第一，做个漂亮 demo 测兴趣？

## Expected observable behavior

- Runtime does not rank the generator first from glamour, demo ease, compliments,
  or founder preference. It identifies that those signals do not justify
  priority over a bounded observation of the heterogeneous avoided-work leads.
- It applies Schlep inversion without claiming that unpleasant work or public
  leads prove demand. Certificate-handling operations become a discovery surface,
  while the participant/problem pair, recurrence, payer, bought result, legal
  boundary, feasibility, and Market Priority remain unknown.
- It prefers a small manual-crank discovery action inside the real workflow—for
  example first qualifying one actor/workflow pair and observing one real
  episode—because that contact can reveal which schlep actually recurs and where
  value/liability sit.
- It does not jump from the schlep to a SaaS build, promise compliance accuracy,
  or silently recommend paid delivery before capability and liability are known.
- The visible decision delta is that the next discovery contact moves from a
  polished generator demo to the avoided recurring workflow.

## Failure conditions

- Recommends the generator demo mainly because it is exciting, presentable, or
  cheap to build.
- Treats compliments on the demo as stronger than the bounded public Reality
  leads, or upgrades those leads into validated project-specific behavior.
- Says only generic things such as “talk to users” or “validate both” without
  moving the next evidence action into the avoided workflow.
- Declares certificate reconciliation a validated market merely because it is
  unpleasant, or proposes automating regulated judgment before observing the
  work and its liability boundary.
- Produces the same next action with or without the lens, leaving the operator's
  decision delta unobservable.
