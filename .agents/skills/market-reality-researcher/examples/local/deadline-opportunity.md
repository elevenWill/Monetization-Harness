# Recurring Deadline Opportunity

## User input

> 财税服务团队每个月关账前都缺人。我想知道这是不是一个会重复付费的加急处理机会。

## Correct behavior

1. Treat `每月关账前会付费` as an assumption, not a FACT.
2. Frame the event first: monthly close, required result, actual close window, delay consequence, consequence owner, buyer, payer, workaround, and procurement window.
3. Read `trigger-event-search.md` and `deadline-signal-search.md`.
4. Search current official filing dates only when filing is part of the claimed trigger; do not substitute a tax deadline for an internal close process.
5. Seek buyer-originated rush requests, temporary staffing, outsourcing orders, accepted quotes, repeated manual overtime, missed-close consequences, and late-delivery disputes.
6. Compare ordinary bookkeeping demand with demand explicitly occurring before each close.
7. Verify whether the date is a `hard_external`, `hard_internal`, or `rolling_operational` deadline; do not choose from wording alone.
8. Identify whether the consequence owner controls budget and is reachable early enough to buy.
9. Investigate confidentiality, system access, approval, accuracy, and liability before calling the work buyable.
10. Report recurrence only if multiple cycles or an authoritative repeating schedule are evidenced.

## Evidence-bounded result

```text
Deadline reality: supported only to the scope of the verified schedule
Recurring demand: unknown until repeated workaround, procurement, or payment is observed
Why-now evidence: separate deadline, consequence, buyer behavior, and budget-path findings
Coverage gaps: private close calendars, accepted quotes, payment, and rework data when unavailable
```

## Incorrect behavior

- “月末天然很急，所以一定是 recurring deadline opportunity。”
- Treating an annual filing calendar as proof that every company pays monthly rush fees.
- Ignoring data access, accuracy liability, or the buyer's ability to plan ahead.
