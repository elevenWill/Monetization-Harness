# Source Strategy

Choose evidence by the claim being tested, not by a universal source score.

## Platform policy

Prefer, in order:

1. current official platform rules;
2. current regulator documents;
3. official learning centers or announcements;
4. credible reporting that quotes and links the rule;
5. vendor explanation;
6. user posts;
7. inference.

Search results and AI summaries are leads only. Open and read the authoritative page. Record `platform`, `region`, `content_type`, `published_at`, `checked_at`, `official_url`, and `status`. If official pages conflict, preserve both, compare effective date, scope, locale, and hierarchy, and mark the older or superseded source accordingly. If the conflict cannot be resolved, use a conditional or blocked verdict.

## Market existence

Prefer independently verifiable transactions or sustained operation, then independently corroborated cases, official merchant cases, complete operator retrospectives, credible interviews, vendor customer stories, marketing copy, and finally unattributed retellings.

Revenue, orders, conversion, repeat buying, retained operation, and customer-side evidence can support existence. GMV alone does not establish profit. Followers and views do not establish a transaction. A provider's case page is normally `vendor_marketing` and `single_source_reported` until corroborated.

## User acceptance

Prefer purchase, repeat purchase, retention, refund, complaint, and abandonment behavior; then verifiable conversion or operating changes; a relevant body of genuine comments; structured research; isolated statements; inference.

Segment by customer, product category, disclosure state, platform, market, and content format. A single negative comment is a risk signal, not a population estimate. Stated liking is not payment evidence.

For content/media, a platform can be a Market Observation Environment as well as
a Distribution Channel. Repeated qualified consumption, return/search/save/share
behavior, persistent substantive comments, and sustained creator operation can
support a scoped audience/value claim. Visible sponsor, affiliate, subscription,
product, or service paths show that a mechanism is offered; use operator or
transaction evidence to establish whether money actually changed hands or the
operation was profitable. Keep platform-policy and distribution-dependency
findings separate from audience-behavior findings.

## Transferability to the current user

Prefer the user's paid replication, then the user's behavioral test, an exact case with closely matched resources, a general exact case, an adjacent case, and theory. Record mismatches in audience, account history, brand, capital, supply chain, platform access, content production, sales, and delivery.

## Commercial demand and pricing

Seek actual price pages, marketplace listings with transactions, proposals, procurement notices, repeated outsourcing demand, operator disclosures, and current substitutes. Hiring indicates resource allocation but cannot alone prove a new product opportunity. List price is not realized price; distinguish advertised, quoted, transacted, recurring, and one-off amounts.

## Purchase triggers and deadline signals

Match evidence to each link rather than treating urgency as one claim. Use an official event owner, regulator, platform calendar, tender, or authorized customer record for the date and scope; buyer-originated requests, procurement, outsourcing, paid rush work, or repeated costly workarounds for action; and penalty schedules, complaints, refunds, SLA breaches, missed orders, or documented extra labor for cost of delay. Identify the consequence owner and whether that person controls or influences budget.

Provider rush listings, discounts, capacity notices, and surcharges prove an offer exists. They do not establish customer-native urgency, an accepted price, or a transaction. Seller landing-page countdowns and scarcity copy are marketing evidence only unless an independent event and buyer-side behavior corroborate them. Reject fabricated urgency rather than treating it as weak demand evidence.

For the source hierarchy and deadline types, read [deadline-signal-search.md](deadline-signal-search.md). For event-first acquisition and buyer/seller origin labels, read [trigger-event-search.md](trigger-event-search.md).

## Source diversity

For decision-critical claims, seek independent corroboration across publishers or channel types. Multiple pages repeating one press release are one evidence lineage. Identify the originating source and avoid double counting syndication.

For login-dependent communities, use only authorized access. Record inaccessible channels as coverage gaps. Do not use a search snippet to claim that full posts or comments were reviewed. In trigger research, specifically name missing private procurement, merchant groups, contract terms, accepted quotes, payment records, and delivery disputes when they block a why-now conclusion.

## Safe capture

Retain only the narrow evidence needed:

```text
title, original URL, publisher, platform, source type,
published/accessed dates, market, claim, brief excerpt or faithful paraphrase,
supports/contradicts target IDs, multidimensional quality labels, notes
```

Put raw temporary downloads in `/tmp`. Never persist cookies, tokens, personal payment data, sensitive personal information, whole pages, full articles, or comment dumps.
