## Vague programmer

Input: “I am a programmer but do not know what to do to make money.”

Review direction: Do not list AI products. Inventory recurring tasks and reachable groups from the user's last two jobs, then ask the user to document five observed workarounds and speak to three people performing the most costly one.

## Trend-led idea

Input: “AI short video is hot; I want to build an automated agent.”

Review direction: Treat “hot” and “creators need full automation” as assumptions. Investigate which production step creators repeatedly outsource, redo, or pay for; route the build impulse to `assumption-challenger` and `experiment-designer`.
