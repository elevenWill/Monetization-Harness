# Business filter: signature decision operators

Source-derived business-verdict transforms, not market evidence or another
universal canvas. Apply only an entry-matched operator.

## 1. Origin test

- **Trigger:** Attention, growth, polish, category status, or price carries the
  argument.
- **Ask:** After removing those signals, can we state plainly who pays, what
  result they buy, why cash recurs, and what maintains the advantage?
- **Effect:** Make mechanism intelligibility a prerequisite to durable
  commitment; resolve the missing payer-to-cash link before optimization.
- **Veto:** “The category is winning” while the mechanism remains unknown.
- **Boundary:** Do not reopen a mechanism already made legible by auditable
  transactions when another gate is active.
- **Source:** preserved source `original-SKILL.md` (“Business Model as Filter,”
  “Return to Origin”) and `references/research/01-writings.md`.

## 2. Validated-late entry

- **Trigger:** Being early, following a trend, or copying a precedent is the
  reason to enter.
- **Ask:** What mechanism did pioneers validate, which errors did they expose,
  and what better evidence-supported position is now available?
- **Effect:** Enter through the improved position or decline the current thesis;
  copying surface form is not a late-entry advantage.
- **Veto:** Inheriting a known business-model weakness merely because the
  category has already been validated.
- **Boundary:** A genuinely evidenced closing purchase window or winner-take-all
  dynamic needs current market evidence, not this maxim.
- **Source:** `original-SKILL.md` (“Follow Smartly, Win Late”) and
  `references/research/01-writings.md`, `05-decisions.md`.

## 3. Thesis ledger and immediate correction

- **Trigger:** New evidence contradicts the original reason for continuing while
  sunk work, prior success, identity, or switching cost pushes continuation.
- **Ask:** What exact business reason authorized this commitment, and does that
  same reason still survive?
- **Effect:** Preserve the reason as dated context, mark the broken premise, and
  stop or reformulate immediately.
- **Veto:** Improving conversion, content, features, or process after the payer,
  bought-result, or repeat-mechanism thesis failed.
- **Boundary:** Do not stop when the thesis survives and evidence shows only a
  repairable execution defect.
- **Source:** `original-SKILL.md` (“Correct errors quickly”) and
  `references/research/01-writings.md`.

## 4. Long-horizon mechanism invariance

- **Trigger:** Temporary attention, arbitrage, subsidy, or one rush transaction
  is treated as durable business quality.
- **Ask:** Which value, cash, and repeat mechanism remains after the exceptional
  condition disappears?
- **Effect:** Separate a capped temporary trade from a business worthy of
  continued investment; require an observable persistent mechanism for the latter.
- **Veto:** Recent growth or a future-category story as the sole durability case.
- **Boundary:** A deliberately capped one-off trade is judged by its own downside
  and economics, not a ten-year test.
- **Source:** `original-SKILL.md` (“10-year test,” “Investing is Farming”) and
  `references/research/01-writings.md`.

## Boundary

FACT discipline, payer-role enumeration, current research, transferability and
readiness, real-offer rules, stop conditions, and Stage routing remain Harness
Core. This lens adds a verdict only when origin, improved late entry, the dated
thesis, or long-horizon mechanism changes it. Otherwise use
`decision_delta: none`. The source is conservative in emerging categories and
cannot prove current demand or durability.
