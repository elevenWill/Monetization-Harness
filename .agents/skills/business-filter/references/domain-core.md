# Business filter core

Derived from the preserved Duan Yongping source snapshot.

## Retained models

- **Business model first.** Ask how money is earned and why the mechanism persists before evaluating polish, price, or popularity.
- **Do the right thing before doing it right.** Validate the business direction before optimizing execution.
- **Return to origin.** Strip away trend narratives and describe buyer, result, alternative, and cash flow in plain language.
- **Circle of competence as cognitive honesty.** “Unknown” is an acceptable result; do not fabricate confidence in a new domain.
- **Let reality validate before following.** Existing payment and workarounds reduce—but never remove—uncertainty.
- **Long horizon.** Test whether the value mechanism repeats rather than merely whether current attention is high.
- **Correct quickly.** When the original reason fails, record the invalidated assumption and stop sunk-cost defense.

## Limitations carried forward

The source lens can be conservative in emerging categories and may overapply long-horizon business intuition. Counter this with short real experiments. Public success stories also contain survivorship bias; treat principles as questions, not proof.
